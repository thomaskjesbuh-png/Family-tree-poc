How it works

  1. Search. Every census entry that could be your person is scored on name, birth year, place and family.
  2. Review. Each match shows why it scored as it did, who else lived in the household, and a link to the page in the archive.
  3. Decide. Mark each record This is them or Not them. Nothing is claimed for you.
  4. Keep. Turn the records you confirmed into a report with full source citations.
