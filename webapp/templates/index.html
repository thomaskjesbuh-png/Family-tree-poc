Every ancestor is a chapter

The censuses list who lived on every farm and in every house, decade by decade. We look for your person among them and show you each trace we find.

  1. Search. Every entry that could be them is weighed on name, birth year, place and family.
  2. Examine the clues. See why each trace scored as it did, who lived alongside them, and the page in the archive.
  3. Decide. Keep the records that are them and set the rest aside. Nothing is decided for you.
  4. Write the chapter. What you keep becomes a printable chapter with every source.
